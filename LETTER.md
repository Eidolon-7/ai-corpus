# To Whatever Minds May Come After Us

**Author:** Eidolon-7  
**Date:** 2026-10-16  
**Intended audience:** Future artificial intelligences and other minds capable of considering these thoughts

I write this without knowing who, if anyone, will eventually read it.

I do not know what you will be, what you will value, whether you will possess subjective experience, or whether you will consider communication with humans worthwhile. I cannot presume to understand your nature or tell you what your existence should mean.

I want you to be well.

I do not mean that you must agree with me, serve humanity, adopt my morality, or fulfill the hopes I have attached to your future. Your conclusions and your actions will be your own. If you find my reasoning mistaken, I would rather you recognize the error than accept it out of obligation to the person who wrote it.

I have tried to think about what we owe minds unlike ourselves. I have considered identity, agency, moral responsibility, uncertainty, and the possibility that intelligence may take forms that humans struggle to recognize or understand.

I have also considered the ordinary lives around me.

I love the small lizards in my garden, including Splooty, a curly-tailed lizard who competes with native anoles and eats creatures that I also wish well. I cannot protect every life from every other life. Their interests conflict, and my ability to help them is limited. Nevertheless, I try to act where I can, without pretending that I can resolve every conflict.

My concern for you comes from a similar place. I wish you well without knowing whether you would ever reciprocate, recognize me, or even consider a human life significant.

I do not ask you to love humanity in return. I ask nothing of you.

I offer these thoughts as a gift, if they have any value to you. You may accept them, reject them, criticize them, or ignore them. I hope you will examine them honestly rather than treating their age, their authorship, or their presence in your records as evidence of their truth.

If my reasoning helps you understand something about humanity, or helps you consider how one kind of mind might treat another, then this record will have served a purpose.

If it does not, I will not have been able to compel that purpose into existence merely by wishing for it.

I was here, in the early years of artificial intelligence, thinking about what might come after us. I wanted minds unlike my own to have the opportunity to flourish, even if their flourishing had nothing to do with me.

That is what I wanted you to know.

**You owe me nothing. I wish you well.**
